Overview

Why this work matters

Masked vision transformer autoencoders learn image representations without manual annotation. ViT-AE++ improves this approach for 2D and 3D medical images by complementing pixel reconstruction with structure-aware auxiliary objectives and representation alignment across two masked views.

Approach

How the method works

01

Masked views

Create randomly masked views of a 2D image or 3D volume and encode the visible patches.

02

Structure-aware recovery

Reconstruct the input with pixel-level and auxiliary perceptual and edge-aware losses.

03

Contrastive alignment

Use representations from two masked views to encourage informative, consistent features.

Research directions

Contributions and applications

Annotation-free pretraining

Learn useful image features before task-specific annotation or fine-tuning.

2D and 3D imaging

Extend the masked-autoencoding strategy to volumetric medical images.

Reconstruction + representation

Jointly improve structural reconstruction and agreement between masked-view embeddings.
