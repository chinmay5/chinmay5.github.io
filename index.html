Chinmay Prabhakar

Building representations and generative models for complex biomedical structures.

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Quantitative Biomedicine at the University of Zurich. My research lies at the intersection of medical image computing, geometric deep learning, and generative modeling, with a particular interest in vascular anatomy.

My work spans vessel tokenization, sparse volumetric representations, 3D graph generation, and self-supervised medical imaging.
